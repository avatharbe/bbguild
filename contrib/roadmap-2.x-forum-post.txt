[i]Status update (2026-07-26): bbGuild core is at [b]2.0.0-rc5[/b] (game plugins: WoW rc3, the other 8 at rc2 — all require core rc5). The extension was rebuilt on the phpBB 3.3 / Symfony architecture with a portal-first guild page, and the game-specific parts were split into 9 separate plugins. Releases are cut as a coordinated train across core + all 9 plugins. The plan now is to ship [b]2.0.0 stable first (stabilisation only)[/b], then start new features at 2.1.0. Project lives at the [b]avatharbe[/b] GitHub organisation.[/i]
